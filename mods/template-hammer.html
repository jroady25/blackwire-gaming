BlackWire Template Hammer.

The Template Hammer, but fast: build and skin your templates in seconds from farther away, using items from your tribe's storage.

Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Builds about 5 times faster than the normal hammer.
  • Auto build up to 100 m away and place templates up to 50 m away (normal: 50 m and 25 m).
  • Skins a 200-piece base in about 3 seconds, with a 5-second skin cooldown.
  • Takes the structure items it needs from your tribe's storage boxes and tames within 30 m.
  • Keeps every piece's own material, and a server-wide build cap keeps many builders from overloading the server.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

Normal hammer vs BlackWire

Craft it at a Smithy or Tek Replicator (level 26) and use it like the normal Template Hammer.

NormalBlackWire
Auto build speed (pieces it tries to place each second)2.540
Auto build range50 m100 m
Placement range25 m50 m
Skin speed (pieces each second)10160
Skin cooldown60 s5 s

Real speed depends on how busy the server is. In practice it builds about 5 times faster than the normal hammer and skins a 200-piece base in about 3 seconds.

Pulls what it needs

Takes missing structure items from your tribe's storage boxes and tames within 30 m of the template.

Keeps materials

Every piece keeps its own material.

Safe for the server

All builders together share a cap of 120 pieces a second, so many builders can't overload it.

Your templates

Your saved and Wikily templates work with both hammers.
