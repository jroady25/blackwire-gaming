BlackWire Structures.

Better functional structures based on Shin's Better Structures: one storage terminal for your whole base, wireless crafting from your dedis, and smarter stations.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation
ServersSingle player · Dedicated
Languages14, follows the game
SetupOptional INI
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Universal Storage Terminal: one screen for every vault, dedi and box your tribe owns within 150 m.
  • Dedicated Storage, plus a Wall Dedicated Storage and Wall Vault that hang flat on any wall.
  • Crafting: Auto Crafter, Kibble Maker, Tek Replicator, Upgrade Station and more.
  • Creatures and farming: Nanny, Hatchery, Gacha Gavager, Crop Handler, Bee Hive and more.
  • Power, defense and utility: Tek Generator, Tek Forcefield, Item Collector, Grinder and bridges.
  • Every window works with mouse and controller, with button hints for keyboard, Xbox and PlayStation.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

What is in it

Many of these work with the BlackWire Dedicated Storage: stations craft from it without wires and send what they make back to it.

The Bio-Grinder needs the Bob's Tall Tales DLC to craft, the same as ARK's own version.

Universal Storage Terminal

One screen for every vault, dedi and box your tribe owns within 150 m. Crafting stations in range can craft from your Dedicated Storage.

Dedicated Storage

1,800 slots. The first item you put in sets what it holds. Deposit All moves every matching item from you in one press.

Dedicated Intake

Pulls your items into the Dedicated Storage boxes near it.

Wall pieces

The Wall Dedicated Storage (1,800 slots) and Wall Vault (350) hang flat on any wall. A normal Vault holds 600.

Resource pulling

Stations pull the resources they need from boxes up to 25 foundations away.

Item Collector

Collects items and can upload them straight into a Dedicated Storage.

Grinder and Repair Station

The Grinder gives back half the resources of an item. The Repair Station repairs gear using resources.

Element and uploads

Element Dust converts 1:1 into Element and shards, and Element can upload to maps that normally block it.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Learn the engrams, craft and place the structures, then hold Use (PC: E, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square) on one for its options.
