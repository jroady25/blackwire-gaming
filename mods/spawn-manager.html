BlackWire Spawn Manager.

Control wild spawns from one admin screen: turn creatures off, swap them, place your own spawn spots, and summon bosses.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation
ServersSingle player · Dedicated
CategoryCreatures & NPCs
SetupOptional INI
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Turn a creature's spawns off, make it rarer or more common, or cap how many are on the map. Tames are never touched.
  • Swap a creature for another, or add a creature to the same spawn areas.
  • Spawn Spots: place your own spots for creatures, eggs, supply drops, crates, veins and more.
  • Boss Terminal: a summoning altar where players pay the tribute you set to summon a boss.
  • Server-wide wild creature amount and wild level boost.
  • Copy your setup to another server with a code (short codes for consoles).
02 · full_guide

How it works.

No restart needed. Changes are saved with the map.

What you can do to a wild creature

Hold Reload as an admin and pick Spawn Manager. Tames are never touched.

ChoiceWhat happens
Turn offIt never spawns, and the ones already on the map are removed.
Rarer or more commonChanges how often it spawns.
CapSets the most of that creature on the map at once.
SwapAnother creature spawns in its place.
AddAdds a creature to the same spawn areas.

Spawn Spots

Place your own spots for creatures, eggs, supply drops, crates, veins and more.

Boss Terminal

A summoning altar. Players pay the tribute you set to summon a boss.

Whole-server dials

Change the amount of wild creatures and give wild levels a boost (10% to 500% of normal).

Copy to another server

Your setup turns into a code you can paste on another server, with short codes for consoles.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

As an admin, hold Reload (PC: R, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square) and pick Spawn Manager.
