BlackWire Hall of Records.

Server leaderboards for every player: biggest tame army, top tames, wild kills, time played, plus your own record card.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation
ServersSingle player · Dedicated
CategoryEvents & PvP
SetupOptional INI
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Biggest Tame Army: the tribes with the most tames right now.
  • Highest Level Tame: the highest level tames ever seen on the server.
  • Most Wild Kills and Most Time Played.
  • My Record Card: your own numbers and where you stand on every board.
  • A message to the whole server when someone takes a #1 spot.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

The boards

Build a Record Monument (level 20), or hold Use on yourself and pick "Hall of Records".

BoardWho is on it
Biggest Tame ArmyThe tribes with the most tames right now, counted every 5 minutes
Highest Level TameThe highest level tames ever seen on the server
Most Wild KillsSurvivors with the most wild creature kills
Most Time PlayedSurvivors with the most time on the server

My Record Card

Your own numbers and where you stand on every board.

Taking #1

The whole server gets a message when someone takes a #1 spot. Owners can turn this off.

PvE and PvP

Works on any server type, for every player.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Build a Record Monument (level 20) and hold Use on it (PC: E, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square), or hold Use on yourself and pick "Hall of Records".
