BlackWire Raid Command.

Everything a PvP server needs in one mod, run from an in-game admin screen. Every part can be turned on or off, with no stat or rate changes.

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Raid hours, purges, war declarations, revenge windows, raid shields and raid claims.
  • Zones with their own rules: Safe Zone, Trade Hub, Fight Zone and Keep Out (anti-mesh).
  • Optional tribe raid windows, no tribe hopping during a fight, and old outpost clean-up.
  • For players: a kill feed, kill streaks, a death recap, a status panel and map marks.
  • A PvP wheel with Base Lockdown, Fill Turrets, bounties, a leaderboard and raid history.
  • A New Season wipe with a backup save and rewards for the top 3 survivors.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Admins can change every number in game: PvP wheel > PvP Admin Panel. In-game changes win until an admin presses RESET TO INI.

Protection timers

Default
New playersProtected for 4 hours, if level 30 or lower when first seen
After respawning15 seconds
Combat lockNo uploads for 120 seconds after a fight with another tribe
Raid claimOnly the claiming tribe can hit the base for 60 minutes
Raid shieldA raided base is safe for 60 minutes after the raid, so it can rebuild
RevengeThe raided tribe can raid back for 60 minutes
Wars60-minute warning, lasts 24 hours, then 24 hours before the same tribes can fight again

When raiding is allowed

By default raiding is always open. Owners can turn on raid hours (default 18:00 to 23:00 every day, UTC-5 = US Eastern standard time) or let each tribe pick its own 4-hour window on the PvP wheel. A purge opens every base, while safe zones stay safe.

Zones

Safe Zone, Trade Hub, Fight Zone and Keep Out (stops meshing). Placed in game, with a 100 m radius by default.

The PvP wheel

Base Lockdown, Fill Turrets (300 ammo each), Claim Raid, a bounty on your last killer, the leaderboard and raid history.

For players

A kill feed, kill streaks, a death recap, a status panel, map marks and a short welcome tour.

Bounties

100 BlackWire Coins by default. Owners can pick a different item.

New Season

Wipe with a backup save, a 10-minute countdown and uploads blocked. Bases in safe zones can be kept and wild dinos are cleared.

Season rewards

The top 3 survivors get coins (1,000, 500 and 250) and a Season Champion trophy.

No tribe hopping

Nobody can join or merge into a tribe while it is in a raid or a war.

Outposts and Discord

Optional clean-up of lone turrets nobody visits, and Discord posts for kills and raids.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Players use the PvP wheel. Admins open the PvP Admin Panel from the same wheel to change every setting in game.
