BlackWire Rewards.

A reason to log in every day: a daily reward streak, a free spin on the prize wheel, and daily and weekly challenges that grow with your level.

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Daily rewards with streak milestones on day 7, day 14 and every 30 days.
  • A free daily spin on an 8-prize wheel, with every prize's chance shown.
  • 3 new daily challenges picked for your level, with 1 free swap a day.
  • A weekly challenge with a bigger reward.
  • An admin page to change every reward, prize and challenge in game.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Admins change every reward, prize and challenge on the admin page. Listed rewards are the defaults.

The daily streak

The Rewards screen opens on your first login each day. Come back every day to grow your streak.

StreakReward
Every dayThe daily reward
Day 7A bonus box of 6 items, double amounts
Day 142 free spins
Every 30 days20 Element

Miss a day and the streak goes back to 1. Your best streak is kept. Admins can turn on forgiveness so a missed day doesn't break it.

The prize wheel

One free spin a day on an 8-prize wheel. Every prize's chance is shown before you spin.

Daily challenges

3 new challenges a day, picked for your level, with 1 free swap a day. You won't get one from the last 7 days.

Bonus spin

Finish all 3 daily challenges and you get an extra spin.

Weekly challenge

A bigger challenge with a bigger reward. It changes every Monday and is never last week's.

What counts

Kills, taming, walking, riding, earning XP, levelling up, play time, supply crates and harvesting trees and rocks.

Admin page

Change every reward, prize and challenge in game, or reset everything back to the defaults.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

It opens by itself on your first login each day. After that, hold Reload on yourself (PC: R, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square) and pick Rewards.
