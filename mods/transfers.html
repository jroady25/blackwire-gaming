BlackWire Transfers.

Take element, shards, boss tributes and trophies to the other maps in your cluster - the things ARK won't let you upload.

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Put items in a Transfer Box and pack everything ARK blocks into one Transfer Pack.
  • Upload the Transfer Pack like any other item.
  • On the other map, unpack it in a Transfer Box and every stack comes back with the same amounts.
  • The box holds 15 stacks. Items that can already upload stay in the box.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

No settings needed.

Moving blocked items to another map

  1. Fill a Transfer Box. Put the items ARK won't let you upload in a Transfer Box: element, element shards, boss tributes and trophies.
  2. Pack them. Look at the box and press Use (PC: E, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square). Everything ARK blocks goes into one Transfer Pack.
  3. Upload the pack. Upload the Transfer Pack like any other item.
  4. Unpack on the other map. Put the pack in a Transfer Box on the new map and unpack it. Every stack comes back with the same amounts.

15 stacks

One box holds 15 stacks, so one pack carries up to 15.

Only what needs it

Items that can already upload stay in the box, so upload those the normal way.

More options

Hold Use on the box for more options.

03 · how_to_use
