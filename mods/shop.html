BlackWire Shop.

An in-game store with BlackWire Coins and player trade booths, opened from your own radial wheel.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation
ServersSingle player · Dedicated
CategoryServer & Admin
SetupOptional INI
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Buy and sell items and dinos for coins. Admins build packages and Mystery Packages in game.
  • Earn coins over time, for kills and with a daily login streak. VIP players can earn more and get a discount.
  • Trade Booths: stock them and set your own prices. Players can buy even while you are offline.
  • Send coins to any player by name, plus wanted orders and trade offers.
  • Admins set prices in game with no restart, with buy limits and cooldowns per item.
  • Coins follow players across a cluster, with optional Discord logs.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

Earning coins

Players earn BlackWire Coins just by playing.

HowCoins
Every 5 minutes online10
Killing a wild creature1
Killing a boss50
Killing another playerOff (0)
First visit each day25, plus 5 more for every day in a row (up to 7 days)
VIP players150% of normal income and 10% off packages

Buying

Items, dinos, packages and Mystery Packages. Every dino has a default price. Bought dinos come at level 150 in a cryopod. Owners can pick a different cryopod.

Selling back

The shop buys items back for 40% of their price. Owners can set this to 0 to turn selling off.

Trade Booths

Stock your booth and set your own prices. Players can buy even while you are offline. The server takes 5% of each sale. By default players can buy from any booth on the map.

Between players

Send coins to anyone by name, post wanted orders and make trade offers.

Prices in game

Admins set prices in the shop's admin screen with no restart. Owners can also set % by category, prices for single items, and buy limits like one 100k XP Scroll per player per day.

Shop items

XP Scrolls (5,000, 25,000 and 100,000 XP) and an Engram Points item (+10), each with its own default price.

Across a cluster

Coins follow players across your cluster, and by default every map shares the same prices and packages.

Discord logs

Optional. Add a webhook and each kind of log (trades, packages, settings, purchases) can go to its own thread.
