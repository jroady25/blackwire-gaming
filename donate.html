support

Keep The Servers Running

Nitrado hosting isn't free, and BlackWire isn't run for profit. If you'd like to chip in, this is where it goes.

Every donation here goes straight to Nitrado — not to a person — and only covers hosting for the games we run: ARK: Survival Ascended, Palworld, and Once Human.

BlackWire's clusters are run by two people, each on their own separate Nitrado account paying their own hosting bill. Pick whichever cluster you play on (or just want to support) below — the link takes you straight to that owner's own Nitrado donation page, so your donation goes to the servers you actually chose.

Before you click through

Nitrado's donation page requires a free Nitrado account to send a donation — if you don't already have one, you'll be prompted to create one. There's no way around this on Nitrado's end; BlackWire doesn't process or see any payment info either way.
