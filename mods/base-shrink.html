BlackWire Base Shrink.

Shrink big tames while they're home so they fit through your doors and pens. They grow back when they leave.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation
ServersSingle player · Dedicated
CategoryBuilding & Bases
SetupNone needed
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Tame Shrink Projector: shrink your tribe's tames in the area to Dodo, Raptor or Parasaur size.
  • Pick an area of 30, 60 or 100 m, or Full size to turn it off.
  • Optional: shrink the tames of allied tribes too.
  • Tames grow back when they leave the area. Put #big in a tame's name to keep it full size.
  • Shrink Collar: a cheap, early item that shrinks one tame to Raptor size anywhere.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

No server settings needed.

The sizes

Place a Tame Shrink Projector and hold Use on it to pick a size and an area.

SizeAbout
Dodo size20% of full size
Raptor size37% of full size
Parasaur size59% of full size
Full sizeTurns shrinking off

The area

Pick 30, 60 or 100 m. Tames shrink while inside it and grow back when they leave.

Keep one big

Put #big in a tame's name and it stays full size.

Allies too

Optional: shrink the tames of allied tribes in the area as well.

Shrink Collar

A cheap, early item. Put it in one tame's inventory and it stays Raptor size anywhere, even after a cryopod.

Ride them

Shrunk tames can still be ridden.

Crafting

The projector is made at a Smithy or Tek Replicator at level 40.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Craft the projector at a Smithy or Tek Replicator (level 40), place it and hold Use (PC: E, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square).
