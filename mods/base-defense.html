BlackWire Base Defense.

Turrets, traps and cameras for your base, all run from one Defense Terminal. Every turret needs power.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation
ServersSingle player · Dedicated
CategoryBuilding & Bases
SetupOptional INI
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • 13 turrets, including Flame, Tranq, Flak, Homing Rocket (anti-air) and a 120 mm Mortar that fires over walls.
  • Defense Terminal: ammo, health and power of every turret, fill or repair all, and modes like Raid, Peace and Lockdown.
  • Ammo Depot refills with special rounds, a Defense Shield dome and a Radar Mast.
  • Traps, plus Security Cameras and Monitors that sound an alarm when an enemy player is on camera.
  • Military storages with raid protection and an optional C4 booby trap.
  • Raid tools for PvP: Turret Jammer and EMP Grenade.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

The 13 turrets

Every turret needs power.

AutoHeavyMiniMinigunTekBallistaCannonFlameTranqRocketHoming Rocket (anti-air)Flak120 mm Mortar (fires over walls)

Defense Terminal

Ammo, health and power of every turret within 150 m. Fill or repair all (40 metal), auto refill and auto fuel, plus modes like Raid, Peace and Lockdown.

Alerts

Tribe alerts, a tribe log, a raid siren and voice alerts.

Upgrades

Each upgrade costs 100 metal and 10 element and gives +20% health and +10% fire rate.

Veteran ranks

Turrets earn Bronze (10 kills), Silver (50) and Gold (200), with +5% armor per rank.

Overcharge and repair

Overcharge costs 20 element and lasts 20 seconds (5-minute cooldown). Turrets self-repair 2% a minute for 1 element per 10%.

Ammo Depot

150 slots, refills turrets with special rounds (Armor-Piercing, Incendiary, Shock) and pulls turret ammo from military storages within 30 m.

Shield and radar

A Defense Shield dome and a Radar Mast that sees 50 m.

Traps

Landmines (600 damage within 5 m), a Trip-Wire Alarm (60 m) and a Sea Mine.

Cameras

Security Cameras and Monitors. An alarm sounds when an enemy player is on camera within 25 m. A monitor shows 3 feeds at once.

Military storages

Crate, Locker and Armoured Vault. In Raid or Lockdown mode a raider who tries one locks it for 90 seconds, and a thief is marked for 10 minutes. Optional C4 booby trap.

Raid tools (PvP)

The Turret Jammer stops turrets within 5 m for 60 seconds. The EMP Grenade hits turrets within 15 m for 20 seconds. Off on PvE servers by default.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Place a Defense Terminal within 150 m of your turrets and hold Use on it (PC: E, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square).
