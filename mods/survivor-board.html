BlackWire Survivor Board.

A notice board for the whole server. Find a tribe, recruit members, buy, sell or plan a meetup - every board on the server shows the same posts.

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Tabs for Find a Tribe, Recruiting, Buying, Selling, Events, My Posts and Replies.
  • Pin a post by picking its type and a few tags - no typing needed on controller.
  • I'm Interested sends the poster a message, even when they are offline.
  • Posts come down after 72 hours, with up to 3 posts each.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

Pinning a post

Build a Survivor Board (level 10), or hold Use on yourself and pick "Survivor Board". Every board on the server shows the same posts.

  1. Pick a type. Find a Tribe, Recruiting, Buying, Selling or Events.
  2. Add tags. A few tags say what you want. No typing needed on controller.
  3. Add a line (optional). A short line of your own text, if the server allows it.
  4. Pin it up. Your post goes up on every board. It comes down by itself after 72 hours.

I'm Interested

Press it on any post and the poster gets a message, even when they are offline.

My Posts and Replies

See your own posts, take one down, and see who is interested in each one.

3 posts each

Each survivor can have up to 3 posts up at once.

Pages

Older and Newer buttons when there are lots of posts.
