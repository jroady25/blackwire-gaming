BlackWire Community.

A custom ESC menu for the BlackWire Gaming servers — Discord and website links, one-tap FPS boost presets, and a quick way for players to grab their own EOS ID.

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • One-click Discord and website links plus scannable codes, also on every player's own wheel.
  • Links can include the player's EOS ID for store and vote pages, and players can copy their own EOS ID.
  • One-tap graphics presets: no grass, no motion blur, longer view distance, an FPS counter and more.
  • Console-safe presets on Xbox and PlayStation.
  • A server info card with your own message and a countdown to the next restart, plus a HUD watermark you can switch off.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Each server sets its own links, labels and buttons in settings.ini.

The buttons

Every button can be hidden or renamed by the server owner.

ButtonWhat it does
SOFT INIRuns a short list of graphics commands. Default: no volumetric clouds, smaller grass, no fog.
HARD INIEverything in Soft INI, plus the lowest shadows, a sharper image, full screen resolution and no debris from broken structures.
RESETPuts the game's normal graphics settings back.
One-tap fixesNo Grass, No Motion Blur, Fix Blurry, No Bloom / DOF, View Distance and Show FPS.
DISCORD / WEBSITEOpens your links. Put {EOSID} in a link and it is replaced with the player's EOS ID, for store and vote pages.
MY EOS IDShows the player their EOS ID so they can copy it.

Console-safe

On Xbox and PlayStation the INI buttons use a shorter list that is safe on console. Owners can set their own console list.

Your own commands

Owners can set up to 10 commands each for the Soft and Hard INI buttons.

Server info card

Your own message plus a countdown to the next restart. Add your restart times and the card shows up. Leave both empty and it stays hidden.

HUD watermark

A small BlackWire crest in the bottom-left corner. Admins can switch it off from the menu.

On every player's wheel

A Community folder on the radial wheel with the Discord and Website links, so players don't need to open the pause menu.

In 14 languages

The menu follows the player's game language.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Open the pause menu as normal. Every player also gets a Community folder on their radial wheel with your Discord and Website links.
