BlackWire Survival Pack.

Auto-eat that only eats the food and drinks you allow, so your taming food is never touched.

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Eats or drinks one item you ticked when your Food or Water drops below the % you pick.
  • Berries, raw meat, eggs and kibble are never eaten unless you tick them yourself.
  • Quick presets: Meats only, Any cooked or Water only.
  • Optional Medical Brew when your health drops low (the server admin can turn this off).
  • Drinks from your waterskin, water jar or canteen, and reminds you to fill it in water.
  • A small note above the hotbar shows what the pack just ate.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

Setting it up

  1. Craft the [BW] Survival Pack. It unlocks at level 3.
  2. Use it to open its settings. Tick the foods and drinks it may use, or pick a preset: Meats only, Any cooked or Water only.
  3. Pick your %. It eats or drinks one item when your Food or Water drops below it.
  4. Keep it in your inventory. It checks every 2 seconds and a small note above the hotbar shows what it just ate.

Taming food is safe

Berries, raw meat, eggs and kibble are never eaten unless you tick them yourself.

Water

Drinks from your waterskin, water jar or canteen, and reminds you to fill it when you are in water.

Medical Brew

Optional: drinks a Medical Brew when your health drops low. Server owners can turn this off.

While knocked out

It stops while you are knocked out, unless the owner changes that.
