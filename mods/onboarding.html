BlackWire Onboarding.

01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • A rules screen with your server name, picture, colour, server rates and links. Players read every rule, then hold to accept.
  • Admins change the look in game (Welcome Screen editor). It is saved and survives restarts.
  • Changed your rules? Raise the rules version and everyone accepts them again.
  • A starter kit admins build in game: items, blueprints and creatures.
  • Up to 5 level kits that players get on their own at levels you pick.
  • Optional Discord posts when players accept the rules or get a kit.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Admins set the look and the kits in game. The INI only holds what can't be typed in game.

What a new player sees

  1. The rules screen. Your server name, picture, colour, rates and links, each link with its own QR code. The player can't move until they accept.
  2. Read every rule. The accept button stays locked until the player has scrolled through every rule.
  3. Accept. Hold to accept, or press once on controller (Space on keyboard) and the bar fills by itself.
  4. Get the starter kit. Items, blueprints and creatures go straight to the player, then a "You're in" screen.
  5. Level kits later. Up to 5 more kits arrive on their own at levels you pick.

Welcome Screen editor

Admins hold Reload and pick Welcome Screen to change the name, greeting, colour, picture, info boxes and button text. It is saved and survives restarts.

Starter Kit editor

Search every item and creature, mod ones included, and add them to the kit. Creatures go in a cryopod, and you can pick which pod.

One kit per player

The starter kit is given once. A dying player is never shown the rules again.

Changed your rules?

Raise RulesVersion and every player accepts again. They don't get the kit a second time.

Your rules

Up to 15 rules in the INI. A | adds a smaller second line and a ! at the start highlights the rule. Leave them empty for BlackWire's 9 built-in rules.

Admins only

Players who aren't admins never see the editors. They only get Server Rules on their wheel.

Discord posts

Optional. A post when a player accepts the rules or gets a kit.

In 14 languages

Every screen follows the player's game language. Text the admin types stays as typed.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Admins hold Reload (PC: R, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square) and pick Starter Kit or Welcome Screen.
