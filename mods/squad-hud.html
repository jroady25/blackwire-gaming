BlackWire Squad HUD.

Live health bars for your tribemates and tames, so you can see who's about to go down in boss fights and raids.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation
ServersSingle player · Dedicated
CategoryGear & HUD
SetupOptional INI
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Shows your tribemates near you and the tames following you, lowest health on top.
  • Downed, Mounted and Far tags, and the row flashes red under 25% health.
  • A compact mode for boss fights.
  • Pick the range, the spot on your screen (any corner or the middle of either side), size and what it shows, with a live preview.
  • Fair play: it only shows your own tribe, unless the server admin turns on enemy info.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

What the panel shows

Press Caps Lock to turn it on or off. On controller, put the [BW] Squad Link on your hotbar and use it.

Your squad

Tribemates near you and the tames following you, up to 200 m, lowest health on top.

Tags

Downed, Mounted and Far tags. A row flashes red under 25% health.

Compact mode

A smaller panel for boss fights.

Place it

Any corner or the middle of either side, any size, with a live preview. Pick what each row shows.

Fast

Updates twice a second.

Fair play

Only your own tribe shows, unless the server owner turns on enemy tame info.
