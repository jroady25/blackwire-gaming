BlackWire Lifts.

Lifts for your base: pick a floor and go. Your tribe and tames ride too, from 1x1 up to 4x4.

Get it on CurseForge Full guideServer settingsHelp on Discord
Works onPC · Xbox · PlayStation

SetupOptional INI
01 · what_it_does

What it does.

  • Lift Stops in four sizes, from 1x1 up to 4x4 (fits a Giganotosaurus), plus a Triangle Lift Stop.
  • Beam Pads to beam to your other pads nearby, and a Lift Crate to send items between stops.
  • A lift control panel with a button for every floor, or press Jump / Crouch to go one floor up or down.
  • Stops link up by themselves and can be named, given an icon and set to Private, Tribe, Allies, Public or PIN.
  • Optional side rails on square stops.
02 · full_guide

How it works.

Numbers are the defaults. Server owners can change them in the settings below.

Riding a lift

  1. Place a Lift Stop on every floor. Stops link up by themselves when they are within 60 m sideways and 300 m up or down.
  2. Stand on a stop. Hold Use and pick Floors to open the lift panel with a button for every floor.
  3. Pick a floor. The ride starts after 1 second. Or press Jump / Crouch to go one floor up or down.
  4. Ride again. There is a 2-second wait between rides.

Sizes

Small (1x1)MediumLargeHuge (4x4, fits a Giganotosaurus)Triangle

Who rides

You, your tribe and your tames together. Tames following you within 25 m ride along, if they fit the stop.

Who can use it

Each stop can be Private, Tribe, Allies, Public or locked with a PIN.

Make it yours

Name each stop, give it an icon, change its look and add side rails on square stops.

Beam Pads

Beam to your other pads nearby. Four sizes, Small to Huge.

Lift Crate

Sends items between stops.

03 · how_to_use

How to use it.

Stand on a stop, hold Use (PC: E, Xbox: X, PlayStation: Square) and pick Floors.
